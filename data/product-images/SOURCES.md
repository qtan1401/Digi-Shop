# Product image and catalog sources

The seed's local image/variant paths are under `data/product-images/`; filenames below are exact. Every new image (products 401-802) was downloaded from the brand's official product page or official CDN, checked by magic bytes, re-encoded with `sharp` (white background, long edge <= 1600px, each file <= ~400KB) and never executed. The retained laptop images (301-305) come from Vietnamese retailer CDNs, as before. Brand images remain the property of their owners; they are used here only as sample catalog data and their licensing must be confirmed before any production use. Prices are sample VND amounts and should be verified before production use.

No product uses a generated placeholder image.

## Processing notes

- Awards/badges that were baked into the official Samsung images (801, 802 heatsink) were cropped out; the Tom's Hardware badge in the MSI image (702) was painted white. Loose switches and spare keycaps were cropped from the Keychron shots (405, 406).
- Samsung and Razer serve small images (about 500px for 701, 801, 802, 504, 602); they were not upscaled, so they look softer than the rest.
- 401 and 501 are wide product shots (about 3:1), so they appear smaller inside the square card frame.
- Monitor variants (701-704) share one photo: the listed "Phiên bản" / "Kích thước" values differ in model code or size, not in appearance.
- Spec sheets and sample VND prices in `prisma/seed-data.js` were written from the official product pages and general market knowledge; verify them before production use.

- All gallery files were re-encoded with `sharp` (flattened on white, PNG, no upscaling, each <= ~420KB; 801 and the Aula/Keychron files are palette-quantised to fit). Where a source carried a text heading or feature icons (Keychron Q1 Max) only the clean part of the photo was cropped out.
- The three 303 gallery photos carried a retailer watermark, which was cropped off (bottom 12%). The two 704 photos were reduced from 2000px to 1156px to fit the size limit. In 601, the light-grey Apple backdrop was brightened to white.
- Small sources were not upscaled and look softer: 301-304 (600-810px wide), the 602 close-ups (363px), 802 (800px), 504.
- 301-gallery-1 and 301-gallery-2 come from the CellphoneS gallery of the 13-inch M3 listing but may show the 15-inch chassis (unverified).
- Several gallery photos are lifestyle shots on dark or wood backgrounds instead of white: 602, 801, 802-gallery-2/3, 405, 406-gallery-2/3.
- 401 and 501 gallery photos are wide product shots (about 3:1 or taller) and appear smaller inside the square frame.
