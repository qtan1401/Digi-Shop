# Product image and catalog sources

The seed's local image/variant paths are under `data/product-images/`; filenames below are exact. Every image was downloaded from the brand's official product page, official CDN or official newsroom (retailer CDNs for the retained laptop shots 301-305), checked by magic bytes, flattened onto white, re-encoded with `sharp` and never executed. Finally every file in this directory was run through `scripts/images/normalize-product-images.js`: trimmed to the product, centred in a 1200x1200 pure-white (#FFFFFF) square with ~9% margin on each side, PNG <= 400KB. Brand images remain the property of their owners; they are used here only as sample catalog data and their licensing must be confirmed before any production use. Prices are sample VND amounts and should be verified before production use.

No product uses a generated placeholder image.

- Awards/badges that were baked into the official Samsung images (801, 802 gallery, 802 heatsink) were cropped out. Loose switches and spare keycaps were cropped from the Keychron shots (405, 406). - 401 is a wide product shot (about 3.6:1), so it appears smaller inside the square frame than the other products; its two gallery shots are close-up crops of the angled Apple photos (right half of the white-key model, left half of the black-key model) so the card does not show a hairline.

- Spec sheets and sample VND prices in `prisma/seed-data.js` were written from the official product pages and general market knowledge; verify them before production use.
